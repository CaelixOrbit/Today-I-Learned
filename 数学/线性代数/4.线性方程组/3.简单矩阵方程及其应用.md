# 简单矩阵方程及其应用

- 设 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 为 $n \times m$ 矩阵，$\boldsymbol{B}$ 为 $n \times l$ 矩阵，则矩阵方程 $\boldsymbol{A} \boldsymbol{X} = \boldsymbol{B}$ 有解的充要条件是 $R(\boldsymbol{A}) = R(\boldsymbol{A}, \boldsymbol{B})$；

- 矩阵方程 $\boldsymbol{A}_{n \times n} \boldsymbol{X} = \boldsymbol{O}$ 有非零解的充要条件是 $R(\boldsymbol{A}) < n$；

- 向量组 $B: \boldsymbol{\beta}_1, \boldsymbol{\beta}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\beta}_l$ 可由向量组 $A: \boldsymbol{\alpha}_1, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_m$ 线性表示的充要条件是矩阵方程 $(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_m) \boldsymbol{X} = (\boldsymbol{\beta}_1, \boldsymbol{\beta}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\beta}_l)$ 有解，即：

  $$
  R(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_m) = R(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_m, \boldsymbol{\beta}_1, \boldsymbol{\beta}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\beta}_l)
  $$

- 向量组 $A: \boldsymbol{\alpha}_1, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_m$ 与 $B: \boldsymbol{\beta}_1, \boldsymbol{\beta}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\beta}_l$ 等价的充要条件是矩阵方程 $(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_m) \boldsymbol{X} = (\boldsymbol{\beta}_1, \boldsymbol{\beta}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\beta}_l)$ 与 $(\boldsymbol{\beta}_1, \boldsymbol{\beta}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\beta}_m) \boldsymbol{X} = (\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_l)$ 均有解，即：

  $$
  R(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_m) = R(\boldsymbol{\beta}_1, \boldsymbol{\beta}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\beta}_l) = R(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_m, \boldsymbol{\beta}_1, \boldsymbol{\beta}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{\beta}_l)
  $$
